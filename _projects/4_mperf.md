## Mobile Network Research at MSSN Lab

**Role**: Research Assistant  
**Advisor**: Chunyi Peng, Professor, Purdue University

### mperf Platform
- Independently developed the initial [mperf](http://milab.cs.purdue.edu/tasks/) platform using Django and Android for remote experiment control and automated cellular-network measurements
- Collected 705 hours of data comprising 4.6 million throughput samples across three U.S. carriers, supporting two published studies

### MMMap
- Redesigned the database and visualization workflows of [MMMap](http://milab.cs.purdue.edu/mapvis/) for 32,000+ network cells and 18,700+ handoff events, achieving a 10× speedup in data analysis and display

### Protocol Security Analysis
- Applied lightweight formal analysis to 3GPP Release 17 IoT protocols
- Identified four vulnerabilities and developed 12 proof-of-concept demonstrations

### Cellular Measurement Studies
- Collected and analyzed 45 hours of UAV network measurements over 1,010 km, using radio-access-network traces to compare airborne and ground-level throughput, latency, and mobility failures
